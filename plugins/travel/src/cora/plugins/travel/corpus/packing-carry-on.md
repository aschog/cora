# Packing a carry-on for a week

## The rule that decides everything

One bag that fits the smallest allowance you will meet on the trip. Check the tightest
airline's dimensions, not the most generous — a bag that fits one and not the other is a
gate fee.

## Clothes

Three tops, two bottoms, one warm layer, one shell. Everything in one colour family so
any two pieces go together. Merino wool over cotton: it dries overnight and does not
smell after two wearings.

Shoes are the bulk. Wear the heavy pair and pack the light one.

## Liquids

Everything decanted into 100 ml bottles in one transparent bag. Solid alternatives —
shampoo bar, toothpaste tablets — remove the bag entirely and never leak.

## Electronics

One charger with swappable tips, one short cable per device, one power bank under 100 Wh
so it may go in the cabin. A plug adapter for the region.

## Laundry

A sink wash every third evening is what makes a week's worth of clothes out of three
days' worth. Bring a small tube of travel wash and a rubber sink plug — many sinks abroad
have none.

## What I stop packing

A second bag "for the beach", a book I will not finish, a towel, and more socks than days.
