## Context

The step was four, the spacing of a classic kettlebell set, and the floor was four with
it. See proposal.md — Why.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals** — any whole kilogram reachable from the weight buttons.

**Non-Goals** — half kilograms, a step read from the sheet, or a typed weight.

## Decisions

**One kilogram, one tap.**

- The step becomes one and the floor with it, since a bell weighs at least that.
- Rejected: a long press for the fine step, which is a second gesture to learn for a
  screen worked mid-set.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Four kilograms is now four taps, which the sheet's own weight makes rare.

## Ports, guards and diagrams

- Nothing of cora's changes: no port, no guard, no diagram.
