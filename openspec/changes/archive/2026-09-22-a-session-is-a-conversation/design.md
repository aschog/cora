## Context

One entity is a `Session` where it is listed and a conversation wherever it is described.

## The name

- The list row becomes `Conversation`: a conversation named by what opened it, with its turns fetched by id.
- The listing becomes `opened()`, which is the docstring's own sentence: a conversation exists here from its first recorded turn.
- `sessions()` and `list()` were passed over: one is the problem, the other says nothing the type does not.
- Path, panel, type and every label follow, so one grep for the old word finds nothing.

## What keeps its name

- `thread_id` stays: it is the model's half of a conversation, and LangGraph's word for it.
- The two halves stay two: the thread the model remembers and the record the reader reads back, dropped together.
- The fitness `Session` and the vocab drill's session stay, because a training day and a pass are sittings.
- `chat` stays a mode of the rail and the Telegram bot's room, and `ChatResult` stays LangChain's word.

## The tab

- CONVERSATIONS is thirteen letters where SESSIONS was eight, and is the one label the rename costs anything.
- CHATS, HISTORY and THREADS were each passed over: the rail's mode, the transcript's window, the model's half.
- Whether the bar takes it is checked in the browser, not decided here.

## The spec directories

- A delta cannot rename a capability, and removing seventeen requirements to add them back would work against the reader.
- So `sessions/` and `conversation/` move on the branch as a docs edit, and the delta targets what then exists.
- `conversation/` describes how a turn runs, so it becomes `turn/` rather than sit a letter away from `conversations/`.
- The archived changes keep the paths they were written under, being dated.

## Guards and diagrams

- A guard in `tests/guards/` holds the word out of `src/cora` and `frontends/react`, so the rename does not drift back.
- The domain map draws `Session` and is regenerated by `make diagram`, which its guard then reads.
- The four flow diagrams draw a turn, so the generator, the Makefile and the guard call them turn maps.
- No port changes shape: `Conversations` keeps its four methods, one under a new name.
- No new dependency, no new seam: a rename through the store, the agent, the API and the page.
