# 4-Week Beginner Strength Plan

Three full-body sessions per week (Mon/Wed/Fri), resting at least one day
between sessions. Warm up with 5 minutes of light cardio and two lighter
ramp-up sets of the first exercise.

## Week 1–2: Learning the movements

| Exercise            | Sets | Reps  | Rest    |
|---------------------|------|-------|---------|
| Goblet squat        | 3    | 8–10  | 90 s    |
| Bench press         | 3    | 8     | 2 min   |
| Romanian deadlift   | 3    | 8     | 2 min   |
| Seated cable row    | 3    | 10    | 90 s    |
| Plank               | 3    | 30 s  | 60 s    |

Use a weight you could lift for two or three more reps at the end of each
set. Focus on controlled form, not load.

## Week 3–4: Adding load

Same exercises and schedule. Add roughly 2.5 kg to lower-body lifts and
1–2.5 kg to upper-body lifts whenever you complete all sets at the top of
the rep range with clean form.

Swap the goblet squat for a barbell back squat in week 3 if your gym has a
rack and you feel confident with the pattern.

## Progression rules

- Never increase weight by more than 10% in a single week.
- If form breaks down mid-set, stop the set — a missed rep is cheaper than
  an injury.
- Deload (halve the sets) in any week where you feel run down or sleep is
  badly disrupted.
