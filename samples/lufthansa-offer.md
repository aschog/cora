# Offer of employment — First Officer, Airbus A320 family

Deutsche Lufthansa AG · Flight Operations · Frankfurt am Main
14 August 2026

Herr Max Mustermann
Frankfurt am Main

Dear Herr Mustermann,

Following your assessment on 22 July 2026 and the simulator check on 29 July 2026, we
are pleased to offer you employment with Deutsche Lufthansa AG as **First Officer on the
Airbus A320 family**, based in Frankfurt am Main.

## The terms

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Position | First Officer, A320 family |
| Base | Frankfurt am Main (FRA) |
| Start date | 1 November 2026 |
| Contract | Permanent, full time |
| Probation | Six months |
| Annual gross | EUR 96,000 in year one, rising by seniority step each year |
| Flight pay | Hourly sector pay above 60 block hours per month |
| Allowances | Per diem at the collective agreement rate |
| Leave | 30 days, rostered in blocks |
| Notice | Three months to the end of a calendar month after probation |

Your existing A320 type rating is accepted, so no type rating training is required. You
will complete the company conversion course — twelve days of ground school and four
simulator sessions — beginning 3 November 2026, followed by line training of not fewer
than 40 sectors under a training captain.

## Conditions

This offer is subject to:

- a valid EASA ATPL(A) with A320 family type rating, and a Class 1 medical with no
  limitation that would restrict multi-crew operations,
- a satisfactory background check covering the last five years, as required for airside
  access,
- proof of the unrestricted right to work in Germany,
- references from your two most recent employers.

Please note that seniority within the company begins on your start date and is not
carried over from previous employment. Your line training is a probationary requirement
and must be completed within nine months of your start date.

## Accepting

Please sign and return one copy of this letter by **5 September 2026**. After that date
the offer lapses and the position is released to the next candidate on the list.

We would be glad to have you with us.
