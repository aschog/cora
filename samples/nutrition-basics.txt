Nutrition basics for strength training

Protein
- Aim for 1.6 to 2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight per day.
- Spread it over 3 to 5 meals; 25 to 40 g per meal is a practical target.
- Good sources: eggs, dairy, poultry, fish, legumes, tofu, whey.

Calories
- To build muscle, eat a small surplus of roughly 200 to 300 kcal per day.
- To lose fat while keeping muscle, eat a deficit of 300 to 500 kcal per
  day and keep protein at the high end of the range above.
- Track body weight as a weekly average; daily numbers swing with water.

Around workouts
- A meal with protein and carbs 1 to 3 hours before training is enough for
  most people; pre-workout supplements are optional.
- After training, a normal protein-containing meal within a few hours is
  fine. The "anabolic window" is much wider than gym folklore claims.

Hydration and micronutrients
- Drink to thirst; urine should be pale yellow.
- Cover micronutrients with varied whole foods first. A basic vitamin D
  supplement is sensible at northern latitudes in winter.
